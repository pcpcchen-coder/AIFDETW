# Candidate-to-role matching rubric

Score each role from 0–100 using evidence from the job description. The score is a prioritization aid, not a claim about hiring probability.

| Dimension | Weight | High-score evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| AI, LLM and agent engineering | 25 | Agents, tool use, RAG, evals, production LLM applications |
| Software and systems engineering | 20 | Architecture, APIs, debugging, deployment, data pipelines |
| Enterprise customer deployment | 20 | Discovery, implementation, integration, customer-facing technical work |
| Manufacturing or energy relevance | 15 | Industrial systems, energy, batteries, EMS/BMS, factory operations |
| Seniority and ownership | 10 | Senior scope, cross-functional delivery, technical leadership |
| Location and compensation transparency | 10 | Taiwan-compatible location, remote/hybrid fit, credible salary data |

## Interpretation

- **85–100: Strong fit** — investigate and consider applying promptly.
- **70–84: Good fit** — worth a closer review of gaps and role scope.
- **55–69: Stretch / selective** — pursue only if the domain or growth opportunity is compelling.
- **Below 55: Low priority** — omit from the short report unless it carries a notable market signal.

For each score, save a concise rationale and list the strongest matching evidence plus any material gaps. Do not infer qualifications that are not supported by the available professional context or job description.
