# Weekly delta reports

Use filenames such as 2026-W43.md.

## Required sections

1. **Scan coverage** — date/time, sources checked, and limitations.
2. **New high-fit roles** — at most five; company, title, location, score, salary evidence, direct link, and one-line fit rationale.
3. **Meaningful changes** — verified status, salary, scope, or requirement changes; show before → after and cite evidence.
4. **Watchlist** — up to five still-active high-fit roles only when useful; avoid repeating full job descriptions.
5. **Market signals** — up to three evidence-backed observations, or explicitly say no reliable new signal.
6. **Next actions** — only concrete actions worth taking.

Keep the report concise (roughly one screen/page). Do not duplicate the full monthly analysis or repeat unchanged details. If no meaningful change occurred, say so briefly. Every factual job claim needs a direct source link.
