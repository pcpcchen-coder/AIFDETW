# Monthly market reports

Use YYYY-MM.md filenames. Produce one report at month-end or when requested, rather than rebuilding a long market overview every week.

Summarize month-over-month evidence for:
- Role volume and seniority mix
- Salary ranges and share of postings with published compensation
- Most requested AI/agent, software, deployment, cloud, manufacturing, and energy skills
- Location and work-mode patterns
- New employers or sectors
- Implications for the job search and skill positioning

Distinguish observed counts from estimates. Include methodology, sample limitations, and source links. Do not infer a trend from a tiny or inconsistent sample.
