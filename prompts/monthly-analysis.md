# Monthly analysis prompt — AIFDETW

Use the job ledger, weekly snapshots, and change log in this repository to produce a month-over-month Taiwan AI FDE market analysis.

1. Load only records relevant to the month and comparison period; do not replay every full weekly report into context.
2. Deduplicate jobs and separate observed facts from estimates.
3. Analyze role counts, seniority, salary transparency/ranges, recurring skills, sectors, employers, location/work mode, and changes in demand.
4. Explain sample size, missing sources, duplicates, and other limits. Do not overstate trends.
5. Recommend up to five practical positioning or search adjustments based on evidence.
6. Save the report to reports/monthly/YYYY-MM.md and cite direct source URLs for important claims.
7. Keep the chat response to a short summary plus the saved report link; do not paste the full report again.
